The concept explores a Delhi-inspired visual identity, easier content discovery, and ways to celebrate the city's literature, heritage and the personalities who shaped it.

The underlying thought is simple: a public library's digital presence should do more than provide information. It should make people curious enough to explore it.

This is an independent concept prototype, not an official DPL project. But I'd love to take it further and contribute to making this vision a reality.
